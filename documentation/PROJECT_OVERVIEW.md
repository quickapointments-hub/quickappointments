# QUICK APPOINTMENTS — PROJECT OVERVIEW

## 1. Executive Summary
**QUICK APPOINTMENTS** is a multi-tenant, dual-role appointment booking platform engineered to connect customers with certified individual and business service providers across 4 launch categories:
- **Salon & Grooming**
- **Healthcare & Wellness**
- **Home Repairs & AC Services**
- **Auto Care & Detailing**

The system coordinates three connected applications:
1. **Customer & Provider Mobile Application** (React + Vite + Tailwind, device simulator + role switcher)
2. **Admin Web SaaS Application** (Executive KPI dashboard, KYC verification desk, dynamic catalog & commission controls)
3. **Centralized Backend API & Database** (Node.js, Express, TypeScript, Prisma ORM, distributed concurrency slot locks, and automated commission fee splits)

## 2. Brand Identity & Design System
Directly derived from the official Quick Appointments logo:
- **Primary Brand Green**: `#65B32E` (Action buttons, active time slots, calendar highlights, Q-slash accent)
- **Charcoal Jet**: `#111114` (Executive typography, dark surface accents)
- **Canvas / Surface**: `#F8FAFC` background with `#FFFFFF` elevated cards
- **Typography**: Plus Jakarta Sans & Inter with monospace figures for slot matrixes
